Me as a Student

My name is Marck Gabriel C. Diaz. I am currently a college student pursuing a degree in Electrical Engineering. I live in San Jose City, Central Luzon, Philippines, and attend school at Saint Louis University. I am dedicated to learning technical skills that will help me build a strong foundation for my future career.

As a student, I am deeply interested in subjects that require logical thinking and problem-solving. I spend much of my academic time studying topics like differential calculus and practicing technical tools such as AutoCAD for engineering layouts and Microsoft Excel for data analysis. I also enjoy learning basic programming in Java to expand my technical abilities.

Outside of my technical studies, I like to balance my education with creative and personal interests. I enjoy studying art history and exploring how visual design principles apply to technical work. In my free time, I like watching and reflecting on films that explore meaningful themes, as well as participating in community activities like environmental cleanups.
